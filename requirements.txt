Objectives:
- The system should track the inventory of different items with names, prices, and quantities
- The system should allow users to interact with the inventory

Functional Requirements:

- Ability to add new products with name, price, and stock quantity
- Update stock when products are sold or restock
- View all products and their stock levels
- Remove products from inventory

Non-functional Requirements:

- Users should be able to provide string input and have graceful error handling
- Users should have an intuitive text menu to work with
- Users should be able to create, edit, and delete stock with no more than three selections of choices
